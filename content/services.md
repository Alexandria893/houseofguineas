House of Guineas provides specialized exotic pet care services across San Francisco and the Peninsula. Our team of veterinary-trained specialists offers two core services: **in-home pet sitting** for clients who want their pet to stay in familiar surroundings, and **boarding** hosted personally by our founder at our San Francisco location.

Both services are designed for exotic pets — rabbits, guinea pigs, chinchillas, ferrets, hamsters, reptiles (bearded dragons, geckos, snakes, turtles, tortoises), and birds (parrots, cockatiels, conures, budgies). We do not care for dogs or cats — exotic-only is our entire practice.

---

## In-Home Pet Sitting

Our team of exotic pet specialists travels to your home across San Francisco and the Peninsula — Burlingame, San Mateo, Millbrae, Hillsborough, Foster City, Redwood City, and surrounding areas. Visits include species-specific feeding, fresh water, medication administration (oral, topical, or subcutaneous injections if needed), enrichment, and habitat monitoring.

### Routine Visits (2x/day)
- **30-minute visits**: $140/day ($70/visit)
- **30 + 60 minute combo**: $165/day
- **60-minute visits**: $190/day ($95/visit)

### Individual Visits (1x/day)
- **30 minutes**: $85/visit
- **60 minutes**: $115/visit

Travel surcharges start from $15/visit for clients outside our primary service area, depending on your location and our specialists' availability. [See full in-home care details →](/home/services/exotic-pet-care-services-in-home/)

## Boarding

Boarding is hosted by our founder, Alexandria, personally at our San Francisco location. Your pet gets their own room with soothing music, dedicated supervision, and the same clinical-grade care our specialists provide on in-home visits. Clients across the Bay Area and Peninsula are welcome to drop off.

- **Exotic pet boarding**: $75/night

A meet-and-greet is required for first-time clients. Pets must be up-to-date on vaccinations by the first stay. [See full boarding details →](/home/services/exotic-pet-care-services-boarding/)

## Why House of Guineas

Founded by an [exotic veterinary assistant](/san-francisco-california-exotic-veterinary-experience/) with experience at Bay Area Bird & Exotics Hospital and ongoing volunteer work at the House Rabbit Society administering RHDV2 vaccinations, House of Guineas was built to bring clinical-grade care into pet sitting. Our team is trained in:

- **Species-specific handling** — prey-animal body language for rabbits and guinea pigs, UVB and basking gradient management for reptiles, dietary precision for birds
- **Medication administration** — oral, topical, subcutaneous injections, eye drops, ear cleaning, syringe-feeding (Critical Care)
- **Health observation** — early signs of GI stasis, respiratory distress, dehydration, and other species-specific concerns most generalist sitters miss
- **Documentation** — daily photo and text updates, weight tracking when relevant

Bonded & insured, Pet CPR + First Aid certified, and a member of Pet Sitters International.
