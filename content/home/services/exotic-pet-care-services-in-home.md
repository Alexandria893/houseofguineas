## Benefits of In-Home Reptile & Exotic Pet Sitting
Your home is your pet's safe-haven. Our [veterinary-informed team](/san-francisco-california-exotic-veterinary-experience) keeps their world consistent while you're away — from precise [reptile habitat management](/reptile-amphibian-care-guide) to gentle small-mammal handling — so there's no stress from abrupt changes.

**Every visit includes:**
* **Pet care** — species-specific feeding, fresh water, enrichment, and medication (topical, oral, or subQ)
* **Home care** — tidying pet areas, plants, mail, and lights/blinds on request
* **Peace of mind** — photo updates every visit, so you can enjoy your time away
