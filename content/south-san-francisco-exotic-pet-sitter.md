South San Francisco wears its nickname on the hillside — "The Industrial City" spelled out in white letters across Sign Hill, looking down over a town that long ago traded its steel mills for the largest biotech cluster on the planet. The people who fill the labs and offices along the bay tend to run on a particular rhythm: long days, conference travel, and the kind of schedules that don't leave much slack the night before a flight out of SFO, which sits just minutes away. When you spend your week thinking in protocols and controls, you don't want to hand your bonded pair of rabbits, your bearded dragon on a strict UVB schedule, or your conure who needs his evening millet to whoever happens to be free. The question that surfaces before every trip is the same one we hear all over the Bay: *who is actually qualified to take care of the exotics?* Conventional boarding kennels are built for dogs and cats, and most pet sitters have never weighed a guinea pig on a gram scale. That is the gap our team fills.

House of Guineas Pet Care is based in San Francisco's Inner Sunset, just over the county line from South San Francisco, and our team of exotic pet specialists comes to your home to provide in-home care. From the hillside streets below the Sign Hill letters to the family blocks of Sunshine Gardens and Westborough, we come to your pets so they can stay in their own enclosures, on their own routines, while you are away.

## Why South San Francisco Exotic Pet Owners Choose Our Team

South San Francisco is a town full of people who think in evidence — scientists, lab techs, clinicians, and the engineers who keep the campuses running. A community built around biotech tends to value clinical rigor, and that is exactly what exotic pet owners here tell us they want in a sitter: someone who brings the same care to an animal that they bring to their own work. Here is what sets us apart:

- **Veterinary background.** Our founder, Alexandria, is an [exotic veterinary assistant](/san-francisco-california-exotic-veterinary-experience) who volunteers with the House Rabbit Society, administering subcutaneous RHDV2 vaccines. Every specialist on our team is trained under that same clinical lens.
- **Species specialization.** We do not sit dogs and cats. This is all we do — rabbits, rodents, reptiles, and birds. That kind of focus is genuinely hard to find.
- **Clinical-grade care.** We can syringe-feed a GI stasis rabbit, weigh a guinea pig on a gram scale, check a reptile enclosure's humidity, and recognize the early signs of a crop issue in a parrot. We also know the local exotic vet landscape, so if something seems off while you are in another time zone, we know where to turn — see our [San Francisco Bay Area exotic veterinarians](/san-francisco-bay-area-exotic-veterinarians/) directory.
- **Flexible scheduling.** Early-morning visits before a commute or a flight, evening visits after a long day, and mid-day medication timing — we build the schedule around your itinerary, not the other way around.

## Exotic Species We Care For in South San Francisco

- **Small mammals** — rabbits (including bonded pairs and trios), guinea pigs, chinchillas, ferrets, hamsters, rats
- **Reptiles** — bearded dragons, leopard geckos, crested geckos, ball pythons and other non-venomous snakes, turtles and tortoises
- **Birds** — parrots, cockatiels, conures, budgies, lovebirds, finches

If your species is not listed, [call or text us](tel:415-484-6493) — if we have not handled it, we will tell you honestly and usually point you to someone who has.

## How It Works in South San Francisco

We start with a free meet-and-greet at your home — a real-time walkthrough of feeding routines, enclosure setup, medication schedules, and your pet's quirks. For South San Francisco pet owners, this usually lands on a weekend or an after-work window, since so many residents are putting in long days on the biotech campuses during the week.

Once you book, we schedule visits around your travel itinerary. Most exotic pets do well with twice-daily visits — morning and evening — so the rabbits get a fresh salad before the day starts and the reptile enclosure gets a proper lights-out check after dinner. We send photo and video updates from every visit, plus written notes on appetite, output, and behavior. If something seems off, we reach out before you land.
