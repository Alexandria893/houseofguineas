##### From Engineering to Exotic Pets

Growing up in San Francisco, I was surrounded by all kinds of pets in my home – rabbits, parrots, turtles, snakes, cats, dogs and even hens (who I lovingly named Flappy and Sweetie)! Even during high school, I couldn't resist spending my lunch breaks hanging out with the adoptable cuties at local pet stores. Maybe that's why finals week at UC Santa Cruz (where they brought in animals to de-stress students) was always a personal favorite; I loved snuggling up with the lovable bernese mountain dog giants. 

I found myself working in a range of roles after college, from renewable energy to technical sales for major companies in the Bay Area. After a few years into my career, I couldn’t shake the feeling that I should be working in a different industry. That calling became very clear when I adopted my amazing pets: first Milo and Luca (guinea pigs!), then Coco and Kai (more guinea pigs!), and finally Chungus and Puppy Dog (not guinea pigs, but are my inseparable bunnies!).

##### Challenges Away from Home as a Pet Parent

The first time I needed to leave home after adopting my adorable crew, panic set in. Finding someone to care for these little fluff balls, with their specific dietary needs and skittish personalities, felt like an impossible mission. It was overwhelming shifting through different sitters, many of whom only cared for cats and dogs. Thankfully, I found a caring sitter, but the worry lingered – were there enough experienced sitters familiar with exotic pets like mine? This experience opened my eyes to the challenges of finding reliable care for my unique companions.

![about](../milo.jpeg)

##### House of Guineas: Born from a Passion for Pets
The occasional trip became a logistical hurdle – reliable and knowledgeable exotic pet sitters were scarce. This experience fueled my desire to create House of Guineas Pet Care: a professional, San Francisco pet care service offering specialized care for exotic pets, and peace of mind for both their owners and companions.
