Southern Marin has a rhythm all its own. Just across the Golden Gate Bridge from San Francisco, the towns strung along the bay — Sausalito with its famous houseboat community and its postcard views back at the city skyline, Mill Valley nestled in the redwoods at the base of Mount Tamalpais, Tiburon and Belvedere reaching out on their peninsula toward Angel Island — draw people who care deeply about where they live and how they live in it. Many of them commute into the city or work from home with a view of the water, and when a trip comes up, the question that surfaces the night before is always the same: *who is going to take care of the exotics?* Conventional boarding kennels are built for dogs and cats, and most pet sitters have never weighed a guinea pig on a gram scale. That is the gap our team fills.

House of Guineas Pet Care is based in San Francisco's Inner Sunset, and our team of exotic pet specialists crosses the Golden Gate Bridge to provide in-home care for Southern Marin households. From the houseboats and hillside homes of Sausalito to the redwood-shaded streets of Mill Valley near Old Mill Park, and out onto the Tiburon and Belvedere peninsula, we come to your pets so they can stay in their own enclosures, on their own routines, while you are away.

## Why Marin Exotic Pet Owners Choose Our Team

Marin is full of people who think carefully and plan ahead — designers, founders, clinicians, and creatives who chose this side of the bridge precisely because it lets them live deliberately. What exotic pet owners here tell us they need is a sitter who brings the same rigor to animal care that they bring to their own work. Here is what sets us apart:

- **Veterinary background.** Our founder, Alexandria, is an [exotic veterinary assistant](/san-francisco-california-exotic-veterinary-experience) who volunteers with the House Rabbit Society, administering subcutaneous RHDV2 vaccines. Every specialist on our team is trained under that same clinical lens.
- **Species specialization.** We do not sit dogs and cats. This is all we do — rabbits, rodents, reptiles, and birds. That kind of focus is genuinely hard to find in Marin.
- **Clinical-grade care.** We can syringe-feed a GI stasis rabbit, weigh a guinea pig on a gram scale, check a reptile enclosure's humidity, and recognize the early signs of a crop issue in a parrot. We also know the regional exotic vet landscape, so if something seems off while you are in another time zone, we know where to turn — see our [San Francisco Bay Area exotic veterinarians](/san-francisco-bay-area-exotic-veterinarians/) directory.
- **Flexible scheduling.** Early-morning visits before a commute across the bridge, evening visits after a long day, and mid-day medication timing — we build the schedule around your itinerary, not the other way around.

## Exotic Species We Care For in Marin

- **Small mammals** — rabbits (including bonded pairs and trios), guinea pigs, chinchillas, ferrets, hamsters, rats
- **Reptiles** — bearded dragons, leopard geckos, crested geckos, ball pythons and other non-venomous snakes, turtles and tortoises
- **Birds** — parrots, cockatiels, conures, budgies, lovebirds, finches

If your species is not listed, [call or text us](tel:415-484-6493) — if we have not handled it, we will tell you honestly and usually point you to someone who has.

## How It Works in Marin

We start with a free meet-and-greet at your home — a real-time walkthrough of feeding routines, enclosure setup, medication schedules, and your pet's quirks. For Marin pet owners, this usually lands on a weekend or an after-work window, and because crossing the bridge takes coordination, we plan these visits carefully around traffic and your schedule.

Once you book, we schedule visits around your travel itinerary. Most exotic pets do well with twice-daily visits — morning and evening — so the rabbits get a fresh salad before the day starts and the reptile enclosure gets a proper lights-out check after dinner. We send photo and video updates from every visit, plus written notes on appetite, output, and behavior. If something seems off, we reach out before you land.

## Marin Areas We Serve

Southern Marin's geography is part of the charm and part of the logistics — the waterfront and houseboats of Sausalito, the redwoods and quiet lanes of Mill Valley climbing toward the base of Mount Tamalpais, the bayside peninsula of Tiburon and Belvedere with its ferry to the city and its views of Angel Island, and the neighborhoods of Corte Madera just up Highway 101. We cover these Southern Marin communities, and for homes out toward the coast along Highway 1 we are happy to talk it through. Whether you are on a houseboat dock or up a wooded hillside, text us and we will let you know if we can reach you.

## Pricing for Marin Visits

- **30-minute visits** start at **$85**
- **60-minute visits** start at **$115**
- **Routine twice-daily care** runs **$140–$190/day** depending on visit length and number of pets

Marin sits north of San Francisco, across the Golden Gate Bridge, which makes it a special travel arrangement rather than part of our regular Peninsula route. Because of the bridge crossing and the drive, a travel surcharge applies, and availability depends on our specialists' schedules. We do not quote a flat surcharge sight-unseen — a houseboat in Sausalito is a different trip than a hillside home in Mill Valley or out toward the coast. Text us your address and dates and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover you and what it will cost.
